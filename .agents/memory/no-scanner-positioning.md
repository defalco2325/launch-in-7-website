---
name: No scanner positioning
description: Permanent product-positioning constraint for Launchin7 website and conversion work.
---

Launchin7 must not include or be positioned around an AI scanner, website scanner, automated audit tool, scanner funnel, or audit-led diagnostic gimmick. AI may appear only as one practical capability within the broader business systems and automation offer.

**Why:** The company’s identity is the premium implementation service itself: focused 7-day Launch engagements, connected growth systems, websites, CRM, automation, booking and payments, analytics, and related delivery work. Scanner-led messaging distracts from that value and was explicitly rejected.

**How to apply:** Keep future homepage, navigation, lead-generation, CTA, and pricing work service-led and outcome-led. Do not add scanner or audit functionality unless the user explicitly reverses this decision.
