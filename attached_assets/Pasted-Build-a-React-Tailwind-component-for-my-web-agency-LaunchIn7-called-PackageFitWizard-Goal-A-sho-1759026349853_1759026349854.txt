Build a React + Tailwind component for my web agency LaunchIn7 called PackageFitWizard.

Goal

A short quiz that helps visitors “See Which Package Fits You Best.” After submit, show a tier recommendation (Ignite/Growth/Scale), then reveal a pricing table + CTAs.

Visual & UX Direction (no color constraints)
	•	Design a cohesive, high-conversion agency look.
	•	You choose the palette, spacing, and typography that best fit a premium, trustworthy brand.
	•	Prioritize legibility, balance, and hierarchy.
	•	Subtle motion and hover states; accessible focus rings; mobile-first; WCAG AA.
	•	Optional: support light/dark themes if trivial; otherwise pick one coherent theme.

Fields
	1.	Business stage (Solo / Growing team / Established)
	2.	Timeline (7 days / 2–3 weeks / Flexible)
	3.	Pages needed (1–3 / 5–7 / 8–12)
	4.	Key features (checkboxes): Lead capture, Online bookings, Payments, Gated content, Blog+CMS
	5.	Copywriting help (None / Some pages / Full site)
	6.	SEO needs (Basic / Deeper)
	7.	Budget comfort (<$1K / $2–3.5K / $5–7K / Not sure)
	8.	Contact (name, email, phone) — required to unlock results

Scoring Logic
	•	Ignite: pages ≤3, features ≤1 (no payments/bookings), copy none/some, SEO basic, budget <$1K, timeline 7 days.
	•	Growth: pages 5–7 OR features include lead capture/blog; copy some; SEO basic; budget $2–3.5K; timeline 7 days or 2–3 weeks.
	•	Scale: pages 8–12 OR features include bookings/payments/gated; copy full site; SEO deeper; budget $5–7K.
	•	If mixed, return the highest matching tier; mark others as “also fits.”

Results Screen
	•	Header: “Your best fit: {TierName}” + small “Recommendation” badge.
	•	One-sentence outcome per tier.
	•	Pricing table (3 columns) with these items:
	•	Ignite (Starter) — Starting at $800
	•	1–3 pages (Home, About, Contact)
	•	Mobile-friendly, branded design
	•	Contact form + social links
	•	Hosting & domain setup support
	•	7-day delivery
	•	Growth (Professional) — Starting at $2,500 (Most Popular)
	•	5–7 pages + custom layouts
	•	Copywriting for 3–4 pages
	•	SEO foundation
	•	Lead capture (opt-in / calendar link)
	•	1 revision round, 7-day launch
	•	Scale (Premium) — Starting at $5,000
	•	8–12 custom pages
	•	Advanced features (bookings, payments, gated content)
	•	Conversion copywriting (all pages)
	•	Blog setup + CMS training
	•	Deeper SEO (keywords, schema basics)
	•	2–3 revisions + 30-day maintenance, PM included, 7-day delivery
	•	Visually emphasize Growth as “Most Popular.”
	•	CTAs below table:
	•	Primary: “Book a 15-min Discovery Call” → https://calendly.com/YOUR-LINK (new tab)
	•	Secondary: “Start My 7-Day Launch” → open a modal or anchor to onboarding (prefill contact info).

Data Handling
	•	Validate contact fields; disable submit until valid.
	•	On submit: save responses to localStorage and call an onResult(data) prop for future backend/email integration.
	•	Show a small toast: “Plan unlocked — check your inbox for a copy too.” (stub email send with TODO comment).

UI Details
	•	Single-page flow with progressive disclosure.
	•	Step indicator (e.g., “Step 2 of 3”).
	•	Smooth scroll to results; skeleton shimmer on results (300–500ms).
	•	Keyboard accessible (Tab/Enter).

Deliverables
	•	PackageFitWizard.tsx (default export) + minimal Tailwind classes.
	•	Self-contained; no external UI libs or tracking pixels.
	•	A simple index.tsx preview that centers the component (max-w container).
	•	TypeScript preferred; clean, commented code ready for Next.js or CRA.

Copy Hints
	•	Top blurb: “Answer 6 quick questions to unlock your 7-day launch plan & pricing.”
	•	Ignite: “Get online fast with a clean, credible presence.”
	•	Growth: “Capture leads with a polished, conversion-ready site.”
	•	Scale: “Scale with premium features and conversion-driven UX.”
